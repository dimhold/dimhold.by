# Review of the ChatGPT proposal

Checked against `RESEARCH.md`. Worth reading because roughly a third of it is
better than what we had, and a handful of its top picks are built on things that
are not true.

---

## Where it is right, and where it is ahead of us

**It self-corrected, unprompted, to the same conclusion the research reached.**
Its second pass says outright: *«я бы не утверждал про многие игры "встречается
только в Беларуси"… правильнее искать игры, которые зафиксированы как
белорусские или имеют особенно характерный белорусский вариант»*. That is our
finding, arrived at independently. When two separate investigations land on the
same correction to the brief, the correction is probably real.

**Its three-tier model is our tier table under different names.** 🟢 familiar
mechanic in a Belarusian shell / 🟡 mechanic fused with a rite / 🔴 locally
recorded in a few villages ≈ Tier 3 / Tier 1 / Tier 2. The 🔴 tier — *build on a
game recorded in a handful of Polesian villages and you own the IP outright* — is
a genuinely sharper way of putting the argument than we had.

**It picked «Пячы ката» as the strongest artistic material, independently.**
Same conclusion, different route. And its narrative treatment is better than
ours: the villagers give **contradictory instructions** about how the cat must be
assembled, so the puzzle is *whom to believe*. That is not decoration — variant
traditions really do disagree, and it turns a documentation problem into a game
mechanic. **Adopted.**

**Four things it brought that we did not have:**

1. **`ethnoby.org`** — a real ethnography mediateka with village-level records.
   The single most valuable item in the whole reply; now queued for mining.
2. **The series structure** — ГУЛЬНІ I, II, III… as separate small games rather
   than one big one, with recurring characters knitting them into one world.
   This fits a static site perfectly and is now the shape of the section.
3. **«Гусі ляцяць» with escalating rules** — the leader names things, you react
   only if it flies, and then the *rule itself* starts changing. Cheap to build,
   genuinely addictive, and «Гусі ляцяць» is attested in the Horki set. ✅
   Attested twice over — **BNT 996** and the Horki set.
4. **«Пярсцёнак» as social deduction** with characters who have tells — the
   panicker, the liar, the thief who passes it on. A real design, not a re-skin.
   ⚠️ **But under the wrong name.** «Пярсцёнак» is absent from the BNT index. The
   Belarusian hidden-token game is **«Золата» / «У золата»** (BNT 991–992), also
   in the Horki set, and Lozka traces eight Dabravolski variants of it plus the
   Lelchytsy «Шкута» and the Rahachow «шута». Build the design — under the name
   the tradition actually uses.

## Where it is wrong

These are not quibbles. Four of its five top-rated concepts rest on errors.

> **Update, 19 Aug.** The BNT 1972 games index — 178 entries, nos. 940–1117 — has
> since been extracted locally, so several of the verdicts below now rest on the
> primary source rather than on argument. Marked ✅.

### «Ваўкі і авечкі» — a name collision, rated ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Its flagship asymmetric-multiplayer concept treats this as a Belarusian folk
game about wolves hunting sheep. It is two different things wearing one name.

- In Belarusian collections **«Воўк і авечкі» is a running chase game** —
  shepherd, wolf, stolen sheep — second entry in the 1991 Юнацтва book.
- As a *board* game, «Волки и овцы» is simply the Russian name for pan-European
  **fox-and-geese**. Earliest citation: a Moscow booklet of **1867**. **No
  ethnographic Russian or Belarusian documentation of it as a folk game exists.**

The concept could still be built — but not as Belarusian heritage.

✅ **From the index.** In BNT the name sits inside an unbroken run of **chase**
games: 945 Воўк · 946 Пасу, пасу авечачкі · 947 and 948 Воўк і авечкі · 949
Пастух і авечкі · 950 Воўк · 951 Авечачка · 952 Авечка · 953 У ваўкі і авечкі.
Nine consecutive entries, every one a running game. No board game anywhere near
it.

### «Гарады» and «Лапта» — Soviet mass sports, not folk games

**Гарадкі** was a Soviet mass sport by **1923**, **лапта** by **1957**. Neither
belongs in a folk-game section without a label. ⚠️ Both *are* in BNT 1972
(«У гарадкі» 1061, «У лапту» 1057 and 1078) — but so are «Піянеры, піянеры» and
«Дзікуны і рускія». That volume recorded what was being played in 1972, not what
was old; presence there proves currency, not antiquity. The Belarusian bat-and-ball game
is **«Апука»**, and Lozka writes plainly: *«У рускіх гэта "Лапта"»*. If we want
the throwing-physics game, the honest sources are **«Паленты»** (whose four lines
are named мета / вароты / двор / хата) or **«Крэглі»**, recorded specifically for
Mazyr Polesie.

### «Жмуркі» as the flagship horror — and the much better game it missed

Blind man's buff is pan-European and carries no Belarusian specificity
whatsoever. But the audio-stealth game it wanted **exists in the Belarusian
corpus already**, and is better:

> **«Браднік».** A blindfolded drag-net fisherman gropes for the "fish". The
> fish **cooperatively call «Глыбока!» and «Мелка!» to steer him** — the quarry
> guides the hunter. When he catches someone he must identify them by voice, and
> **the catch answers with the name of a fish** rather than their own.

Sonar plus a recognition duel, recorded, and with a genuinely strange
cooperative-prey rule that no one has built. This is the single best correction
to come out of the comparison. **Replaces «Жмуркі» outright.**

### «Міхасік» — the weakest link, and it carries a whole tier

It is offered as the model 🟢 case, *«широко фиксируется именно как белорусская
народная»*. The source given is a music-teacher's blog. **«Міхасік» is a modern
pedagogical adaptation** — musical chairs in лапці — and does not appear in the
pre-Soviet or academic collections. Using it as the worked example of "a folk
game with a Belarusian shell" undermines the tier it is meant to demonstrate.

✅ **From the index.** BNT 1972 lists 178 games and **«Міхасік» is not among
them** — although the volume cheerfully includes «Штандар, стой!», «Дзікуны і
рускія» and «Піянеры, піянеры», so it plainly was not filtering out recent
arrivals. A game missing from a collection that permissive is very unlikely to
be traditional.

### It missed the strongest fact about its own favourite

Nowhere does it note that **«Ката пячы» is on the State List of Historical and
Cultural Values, 33АК000117**, carried by the Redźka family of Skirmantava and
run annually since 1993. It calls it *«скорее игровой обряд»* — hedging on the
one game in its list whose status is documented and unambiguous.

### Sourcing, generally

`muzruk.net`, «Маленькая Страна», ru.wikipedia. Tertiary pedagogical material,
which is exactly the layer where Soviet PE inventions and genuine folk games sit
side by side, indistinguishable. That is how «Міхасік» got promoted and how
«гарадкі» slipped in. The research here used the state heritage databank, the
intangible-heritage inventory, a peer-reviewed article from Polack State
University, and the full texts of the BNT volumes.

### Scope

Most of its highest-rated concepts — asymmetric multiplayer, social deduction
with live players, co-op procession — need a backend and a player base. This
site is static, on GitHub Pages, and will stay that way. Its own second pass
corrects this and moves to single-player browser games; that half of the reply is
the usable one.

## The methodological difference

The GPT text **starts from genres and looks for folklore to fill them**: Dead by
Daylight needs an asymmetric game, so wolves-and-sheep is recruited; horror needs
blindness, so жмуркі is recruited. The research went the other way — read what
was recorded, then ask what it already is.

The first method is how you end up with what its own closing paragraph warns
against: *«обычное фэнтези со славянскими декорациями»*. The corrective is in
that same closing paragraph and it is the best line in the whole reply — look at
**the rules, the regional variants and the songs**, because that is where
mechanics live that nobody else has.

Which is precisely where «Браднік»'s steering calls, «Грахі»'s guilt economy,
«Мянькі»'s reward-the-longest-sheltered scoring, and «Панначка»'s
relight-the-circle ending came from.

## What we take

**Adopted:**
- The **series** structure with recurring characters — now the shape of the section.
- **Contradictory-instructions** puzzling inside Ката пячы.
- **«Гусі ляцяць»** and **«Пярсцёнак»** added to the catalogue as candidates,
  pending verification of their ethnographic depth.
- **`ethnoby.org`** as a primary source to mine.
- The 🔴 argument: locally recorded beats famous.

**Rejected:** «Ваўкі і авечкі», «Гарады», «Лапта», «Жмуркі», «Міхасік» —
each for a stated reason above.

**Superseded:** «Жмуркі» → **«Браднік»**.
